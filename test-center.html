The Test Center

What it is, why we built it, and how to get the most from it

Your Magnolia portal now has a Test Center. It keeps every test and quiz you write this year in one place, sorted by unit, and turns each one into a blank copy you can redo and an answer key you can check yourself against.

1.Why we built it

A marked test shows exactly what a student understands and what they don't. Going over tests during the semester is hard, though. Tutoring time is limited, the course keeps moving, and tests often come back weeks after they were written, when the class is already two units further on.

So tests tend to pile up until January, when there is too much to review and too little time. The Test Center is our answer: upload each test when you get it back, and it is filed and ready to redo whenever you have the time.

Redoing old tests is one of the best-studied ways to remember material. Practising recall works better than rereading,1 and spreading that practice over weeks works better than cramming it into a few days.2 It also fits how we tutor: we want students doing the thinking themselves, not watching it done.3

2.Three ways to use it

  1. Quizzes, to get ready for tests. Before a unit test, make a practice copy of that unit's quizzes: a new quiz on the same skills, with different questions and its own answer key.
  2. Tests, to get ready for exams. Every unit test you upload becomes a blank copy to redo before the exam.
  3. Keeping your tutor in the picture. Uploading through the semester, not just at the end, shows us how the course is going, which units went well and which need another look, while there is still time to act on it.

3.How it works

  1. Upload your syllabus first. A photo or PDF of your teacher's course outline sets your units to match your class. Until then, math, biology and chemistry units follow the Ontario curriculum, and for other subjects you add your own.
  2. Upload each test or quiz you get back. From the Test Center, choose Upload a test, then take a photo of each page (they are combined into one file) or add a PDF.
  3. It sorts itself. We work out whether it is a test or a quiz, which unit it belongs to, its title, and your mark if one is written on it. You can change any of these.
  4. You get a blank copy and an answer key. Within a few minutes, every upload has a clean blank copy to redo and an answer key with the work shown.
  5. Make a practice copy for more practice. It writes a new version of the test with different questions on the same skills, plus its own answer key, in a minute or two.
  6. Flag anything you are stuck on. Tap a question's number and your tutor sees it before your next lesson, so you can go over it together.

The blank copies and answer keys are made by AI. They are usually right, but not always. If a question or an answer looks wrong, open “Does a question look wrong?” or “Does an answer look wrong?” under it and tap the number. It helps us fix it.

4.For parents

Parents see the same Test Center in their own portal and can upload tests on their child's behalf. The first time anyone opens it, a short tour shows how it works; you can replay it at any time with How it works.

5.Common questions

What can I upload?
Photos (JPG or PNG, up to 12 pages at once) or a PDF, up to 10 MB, one test or quiz per upload. A long scrolling screenshot works too; it is split into pages for you.
How many practice copies can I make?
Up to 3 of each test, and up to 5 a day.
What if a test can't be read?
It shows as “We couldn't read this one.” Remove it with the × beside it, then try a clearer photo, or a PDF.
Can I remove a test?
Yes. Tap the × beside it and confirm. It is gone for good, with its blank copy, answer key and practice copies, and your tutor won't see it either. You can't remove a test while it is still being prepared; try again in a few minutes.
Who can see my tests?
You, your parent, your Magnolia tutor and the Magnolia team. Your tutor can see your blank copies and the questions you flag.
Why are some of my tests in “Not in a unit yet”?
That happens when a test doesn't clearly match a unit, or after a new syllabus changes your units. Choose where each one belongs with Choose a unit.
