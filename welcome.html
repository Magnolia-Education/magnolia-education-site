What We Do

It's about math. It's more than math.

Your child meets the same tutor at the same time every week on a shared whiteboard. Sessions are usually 45 minutes. They stay with the same tutor for the school year.

Sessions use a shared whiteboard, so your child needs a tablet and stylus to write math. This XP-Pen works well and is what most of our students use.

Homework and Tests

Your nudges help

Your child is responsible for both, but your nudges help make it consistent. It really depends on the student.

  • Mark their homework. This is teacher-assigned homework. They should be marking it themselves, but a nudge from you goes a long way.
  • Upload tests in the portal as they get them. We use these for exam prep and to keep track of what they're struggling with.

Getting started

  1. Fill out the onboarding form
  2. Make sure your child has a tablet and stylus for their sessions. Need one? Here's what we recommend.
  3. You'll receive a sign-in link to your Magnolia portal
  4. Add your child's availability
  5. We match your child with a tutor, open a group chat with you, your child, and your child's tutor, and confirm your first session date and time
The group chat is where all scheduling and communication happens. Please keep everything there so all relevant parties can see it.
