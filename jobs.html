About Magnolia Education

Magnolia Education is a Toronto math and science tutoring company founded by Rachit Chakerwarti. We work with students from Grade 6 to Grade 12 on the Ontario curriculum, through weekly tutoring, exam prep and prep courses, mostly online. Our promise is better grades and a better way to get them. Alongside the subject material, we teach study skills: how to prepare, how to practise and how to learn from every quiz and test, so students build habits that last. We're also building our own software, which other tutoring businesses will soon run on too.

Administrative Assistant (Part-Time)

Remote · Part-time, flexible schedule · 5 to 15 hours per week, with potential to increase · $25 per hour, with a compensation review after one year and a path to $30 per hour · Subject to a probationary period

Position summary

Magnolia Education is seeking a part-time Administrative Assistant to support our founder, Rachit Chakerwarti, with parent and tutor communications, operational administration and executive support. This position requires a high degree of responsiveness: timely replies to parents and tutors, and prompt escalation of any matter requiring Rachit's attention.

The role also includes personal and executive administrative support, including project tracking, deadline management and the coordination of ongoing priorities. Responsibilities in this area will vary according to business needs.

Key responsibilities

  • Manage the company inbox and text message line, responding to parent inquiries on Rachit's behalf
  • Escalate complaints, sensitive matters and unresolved questions to Rachit in a timely manner
  • Coordinate tutor matching by issuing offers to tutors selected by Rachit
  • Conduct follow-ups with parents and tutors to ensure all communications are resolved
  • Audit communications to identify and address unanswered messages or outstanding commitments
  • Maintain and track administrative tasks in the company task queue
  • Provide personal and executive administrative support to Rachit, including project and deadline tracking

Future responsibilities

  • Develop, document and maintain standard operating procedures as processes are formalized
  • Coordinate and support tutors, including check-ins, task follow-up, and the administration of surveys
  • Support partner tutoring businesses following the launch of the Magnolia platform

Process improvement

This role carries the authority to identify which parts of the work should be automated. The Administrative Assistant will flag repetitive tasks for automation and work with Rachit and our AI tools to remove them, so that the role can focus on the work that benefits most from a human touch: judgment, warmth and relationships with families and tutors. As our platform grows, the Administrative Assistant will help shape the admin experience for other tutoring businesses that run on it.

Qualifications

  • Consistent availability to respond to messages promptly during working hours
  • Excellent written communication skills, with a professional and personable tone
  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
  • Adaptability in a fast-changing environment where processes are still being established
  • An interest in improving processes and working with AI tools
  • Experience writing or following standard operating procedures is an asset
