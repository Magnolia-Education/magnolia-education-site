Prep courses

Small group courses that review the previous year and get ahead on the next one, before the semester starts. Sessions are a full hour and the whole block is prepaid up front.

Semester block

1 session/week through the semester$70/session
Per student, 2 to 4 per cohort1 hour

Summer block

3 sessions/week, 12 sessions$70/session
July or August term1 hour
  • The number of sessions depends on when you start. Blocks run to the end of the term, so a later start means fewer sessions, and the block is priced on the sessions you actually get.
  • Two students counts as a cohort. A pair is the small end of the group, at the same $70 per student per session, so bringing a friend in the same course is the cheapest way to do prep.
  • Both students are billed for every session in the block, whether or not both attend. If one withdraws partway, the other moves to one‑on‑one pricing from the next block.
  • Prep one‑on‑one runs at the weekly tutoring rates above.
  • Blocks are prepaid for the course, not month by month, because a block is a fixed number of sessions with an end date.

Courses currently offered: Grade 11 Functions and Grade 12 Advanced Functions. More about prep cohorts.

Billing

  • E‑transfer to hello@magnolia‑education.com is the standard method.
  • Weekly tutoring is prepaid monthly, due before the first session of the billing period.
  • Your credit card is on file for security only and is not charged for regular billing. A 3% convenience charge applies if it is used.
  • Mock exams and prep blocks are billed separately from monthly sessions.

Cancellations, rescheduling, makeup credits and holidays are all set out in the tutoring policies.

Questions about these policies? Reach out to your tutor or contact us at hello@magnolia-education.com.
